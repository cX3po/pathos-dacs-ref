# Agreement commitment and bundle finalization adapters

These adapters implement the DACS-3 v0.4 finality commitment and DACS-5 v0.4 evidence-bound bundle surfaces pinned to DACS-Standard `63793a39`.

## Implemented rules

`commitAgreement` implements DACS-3 artifact selection and shape, canonical agreement hashing and buyer/seller signing, and checks 1–9 of §8.5.2 for the supported `commit-agreement` path. It recomputes the listing hash from the JCS signed scope with `contentHash` and `signature` omitted. For `pay-alternative`, the selected rail must canonically match exactly one signed alternative (APR-3); `acceptedRails` does not substitute. It checks CD-1 currency/price validation, deliverable equality, receipt-relative deadline and expiry, negotiation pattern, and SE-8 demand/procurement direction. `commit-payee-bound-agreement` and `priorPaymentDispositionRef` replacement disposition (APR-5/APR-6) are refused with typed `NotSupportedError`.

The commitment is a `FinalityCommitmentRecord`, signed by the authenticated orchestrator under `dacs-finality-commitment:v1:` and anchored at `dacs3:commit:{jobId}` (CA-1–CA-9). The adapter independently re-fetches the signed agreement and commitment record, verifies CORE §5.1 receipt fields (SR2-4–SR2-7), and hashes recoverable receipt evidence bytes; receipt evidence without recoverable bytes is refused. It requires the provider to report `state:"finalized"` and derives `committedAt` from `blockRef.timestamp`. A cold `pass` means “the injected receipt provider attested finalization and every offline-checkable field agrees”; it does not mean substrate-proven finality. Cold verification requires an injected signature verifier and exactly one valid buyer and seller agreement signature (CA-6/CA-7).

`finalizeBundle` verifies the listing signature and recomputes the effective pipeline. A `pay-alternative` rail comes from the resolved agreement carried by the finalized commitment and is projected by the required `projectPaymentRail` dependency. Before emission of `completed` or `failed-counterparty`, `fetchCommitment` must resolve the commitment, agreement, and finalized receipt; hashes and the orchestrator, buyer, and seller signatures are verified (ST-11). One shared evidence function is used by emission and cold verification; it covers the SEB checks listed next and does not implement ST-8 supersession records. It resolves each executed payment/delivery result, checks exact references, shape, job/index/kind/outcome, signature, orchestrator authorship, receipt binding, and writer identity. Payment logical addresses are recomputed as `dacs4:payment:{jobId}:{railId}:{phaseIndex}` (PC-2) with `railId` taken from the projected rail parameter of a pay-alternative phase or else the concrete pay kind and percent-encoded as a CF-4 segment; caller input is only compared with that value.

The default output is `EvidenceBoundFaultAttestationBundle`, signed under `dacs-evidence-bound-fault-bundle:v1:`. `kind:"fab"` selects `FaultAttestationBundle` and `dacs-fault-bundle:v1:`. Buyer and seller sign every non-abort output; a distinct injected orchestrator also signs. Role-specific copies are anchored at `stor-{sha256(jobId + "-bundle-" + role)}` and, when `publishBundleBinding` is supplied, a signed `BundleBinding` is published for each write (BB-1–BB-8). `bundleVersion:"1"` is never emitted. The verifier dispatches by the mutually exclusive discriminator while preserving legacy reads.

## Local decisions and conventions

1. A bundle's `agreementRef` references the finalized DACS-3 commitment record required by ST-11. The commitment carries `agreementHash`; an agreement artifact reference also carries that content hash where its schema permits.
2. The optional agreement-artifact anchor uses `dacs3:agreement:{jobId}` as a local implementation convention, not a DACS logical-address assignment. The mandatory commitment address is `dacs3:commit:{jobId}`.
3. EBFAB is the default; `kind:"fab"` is the explicit compatibility option. Legacy `bundleVersion:"1"` is read-only.
4. Top-level evidence references are emitted in executed phase-index order. This is a local deterministic rule; DACS requires exactness and preserves array order but does not prescribe a general sort.
5. `src/demos/storage.ts` does not prove CORE finality. The agreement adapter injects `receiptProvider`. Bundle emission and cold verification require both `fetchCommitment`, which resolves the stored commitment and agreement bytes plus their anchor metadata, and `fetchReceipt`, which independently resolves the receipt by the commitment logical address and every bundle/evidence logical address. A bundle cold `pass` means both seams resolved, the receipt evidence reproduced each stored-content hash, and all offline-checkable signatures, bindings, and receipt-state rules passed (every receipt `finalized` for the `completed` outcome; `included` accepted for other outcomes, so a non-completed pass attests inclusion, not finality); an agreement cold `pass` has the corresponding meaning for `receiptProvider`. Neither means substrate-proven finality. A missing commitment seam for an ST-11 outcome is `indeterminate` with `commitment-not-refetched`; a thrown commitment-resolution transport error is also `indeterminate`. Once a commitment resolves, a missing or contradictory receipt is `fail`, while an independently observed `included` commitment is `indeterminate` with `commitment-pending`. Without the bundle receipt seam, or when an evidence receipt cannot be refetched, cold verification returns `indeterminate` with `evidence-not-refetched`; a fetched contradictory receipt is `fail`.
6. `agreementRef.contentHash` is the SHA-256/JCS hash of the signed agreement bytes actually anchored. `agreementRef.unsignedContentHash` retains the agreement signed-scope hash.
7. Evidence authorship is established cryptographically: the evidence signer and receipt writer must both equal the authenticated orchestrator for that phase by canonical claim key.
8. Cold bundle verification requires buyer and seller copies, equal signed-scope hashes, both party signatures, finalized commitment resolution when required, and a SEB evidence refetch. An orchestrator copy is optional and is verified when present.
9. The shared verifier dispatches among legacy, FAB and EBFAB discriminators and matching signing domains; legacy tests remain supported.
10. The pinned `bundle-settlement-evidence-bijection-v0.4`, `commitment-record-compatibility-v0.1`, and `phase-kind-divergence-v0.3` corpora are byte-integrity-only because they contain abstract or partial decision-model inputs rather than complete adapter inputs. The agreement-listing, EBFAB, settlement-evidence, commitment-authority, and BundleBinding fixtures receive their applicable semantic, hash, or signature replay checks.

## Out of scope

Payee-bound agreements, APR-6 replacement disposition, ST-8 supersession graphs, a live Demos CORE receipt provider, live gateway wiring, private/encrypted agreement retention, non-Ed25519 signing implementations, pointer publication/fetching, and retries of anchor/broadcast operations are outside this change. The adapters do not modify `src/live/organ-gateway.mts`.
